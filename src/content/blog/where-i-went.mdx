The last post here went up on September 23, with beta.2. RC.1 was supposed to follow in October.
It came out today instead, as `26.11.0-rc.1` on npm and JSR. That gap deserves an explanation, so
here it is.

## My job needed more of me

Green Tea gets built in the hours my job leaves free. This autumn the workload there went up, and
those hours went with it. Nothing dramatic. There was just less time, and less of me left at the
end of the day to spend on a framework.

## Autistic burnout

This is the harder part to write. I'm autistic, and when the load stays high for long enough I hit
a kind of burnout that a weekend off doesn't fix. Things that are normally easy, like opening the
editor or reading an issue, start costing more than I have. What works for me is stepping back for
a while.

Being the only developer on Green Tea makes that complicated. There's no one to hand the work to,
so when I stop, the project stops. I decided to accept that. I don't want to build a framework that
ships on schedule because its maintainer pushed through burnout, and I wouldn't ask anyone to
depend on one.

## The No Names Sound Machine

The rest of the time went into my music project, [The No Names Sound Machine](https://thenonames.org/en/),
which was overdue for a release. Yes, yes, there's probably some AI slop in my soup. If you
want to listen anyway, it's on the site, or find it on Spotify, or wherever you get your music.

## Why I didn't just ship it

I could have pushed a tag at any point and called it a release candidate. But an RC only means
something if the API has actually stopped moving, so the freeze had to be real before the tag
went out.

So here is what RC.1 promises. The public API of `@green-tea/core`, `/deno`, `/bun` and `/edge` is
frozen. From now until stable, releases are fixes only: nothing gets added, removed, renamed or
narrowed. That isn't a promise you have to take on trust. Every entry point has a committed API
report, and CI fails when the code drifts from it. Mesh is still experimental and stays outside the
promise. The full policy is on the [stability page](https://green-tea.expressive-tea.io/docs/stability/).

## What happens now

Fixes until stable, at a pace I can sustain. Development has also moved into the open: it happens
on [GitHub](https://github.com/Expressive-Tea/green-tea) now, so issues and pull requests go there.
If something in RC.1 breaks for you, that's the place to say so.
